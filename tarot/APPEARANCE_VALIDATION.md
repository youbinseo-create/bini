# Appearance Validation

Created: 2026-05-15

## Scoring Formula

Use this formula for visual production decisions:

```text
Face appeal: 35
Card-specific identity: 25
Format pass: 25
Deck cohesion: 15
Total: 100
```

Why:

```text
A beautiful card that fails deck format is not a final master.
A perfectly formatted card with a forgettable person is not fundable enough.
The winning card must do both.
```

## Korean Costume Risk Review

User correction:

```text
옷차림에 유의해줘. 중국풍이 아니라 한국풍으로 만들어야해.
```

Review:

- Some current cards risk reading as generic East Asian fantasy because of long floating sleeves, ornate hair ornaments, and decorative architecture that can look Chinese.
- `Suho S1` and `Gijun S1` especially need inspection before final master use because some background ornaments can read as writing-like or Chinese-style palace detail.
- Future production must use explicit Korean clothing and setting vocabulary, not vague "East Asian" styling.

New scoring penalty:

```text
Korean costume/setting authenticity is now a gate.
If a card reads as Chinese hanfu, wuxia, xianxia, or generic East Asian fantasy, it cannot be a final master even if the face and format are strong.
```
