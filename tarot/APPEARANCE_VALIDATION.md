# Appearance Validation

## Scoring Formula

Use this formula for visual production decisions:

```text
Face appeal: 35
Card-specific identity: 25
Format pass: 25
Deck cohesion: 15
Total: 100
```

Why:

```text
A beautiful card that fails deck format is not a final master.
A perfectly formatted card with a forgettable person is not fundable enough.
The winning card must do both.
```

## Judgment

### Gyeoldan S4

Strongest commercial image in this batch.

Reasons:

- Attractive face and full-body silhouette are memorable.
- Door-opening action communicates decision/courage clearly.
- Bright adventure mood fits the self-reflection product direction.
- Exact `1024 x 1536` format pass.

Risk:

- It is close to a fashion hero image, so future cards should not all become this same romantic male archetype.

### Chiyu S4

Strong candidate.

Reasons:

- Attractive, calm, trustworthy healer.
- Water-pouring gesture communicates healing immediately.
- Bright garden/apothecary setting is positive and fundable.
- Exact `1024 x 1536` format pass.

Risk:

- Needs more unique face differentiation if many male cards use the same soft handsome look.

### Seolgye S4

Good face improvement, but not final.

Reasons:

- Strategist looks more attractive than S3.
- Planning table and model-building gesture work.

Problems:

- Failed exact format: `992 x 1586`.
- Mood is slightly darker and heavier than ideal.
- Needs cleaner luxury-planning aura, less crowded machinery.

### Inyeon S4

Warm and likeable, but too safe.

Reasons:

- Friendly mature monk face is appealing.
- Positive relationship mood is clear.

Problems:

- Failed exact format: `992 x 1586`.
- Pose is too expected.
- Needs a more unforgettable face or gesture to compete with the stronger cards.

## Production Decision

Next production priority:

```text
1. Regenerate Seolgye with exact format and brighter, more luxurious strategist appeal.
2. Regenerate Inyeon with exact format and a more memorable mediator gesture.
3. Keep Chiyu S4 and Gyeoldan S4 as current top candidates.
```
